# Musme brand guidelines

## Foundation

Musme should feel intelligent, calm, exact, and useful. The brand avoids generic AI futurism and instead communicates operational clarity, quiet confidence, and human-centered technology.

## Logo

The approved logo is the supplied mint gradient mark. Treat it as artwork rather than a character or replaceable typeform.

- Minimum digital height: 24 px.
- Preferred navigation height: 30-34 px.
- Clear space: at least 25% of the visible mark height on every side.
- Preferred backgrounds: charcoal `#0D0F0C`, soft white `#F2F5F1`, or neutral photography with strong contrast.
- Do not stretch, crop through the mark, apply shadows, recolor, rotate, outline, or add effects.

## Color

### Approved source color

- Musme Mint: `#6BEFBC` / RGB `107, 239, 188`

### Functional extensions

These colors are recommendations derived from the approved mint for interface accessibility and hierarchy.

- Mint Light: `#A8F8DA` / RGB `168, 248, 218`
- Mint Dark: `#2EAE84` / RGB `46, 174, 132`
- Mint Ink: `#08271D` / RGB `8, 39, 29`
- Charcoal: `#0D0F0C` / RGB `13, 15, 12`
- Elevated Charcoal: `#151815` / RGB `21, 24, 21`
- Soft White: `#F2F4EE` / RGB `242, 244, 238`
- Muted Silver: `#A1A89D` / RGB `161, 168, 157`

Use one mint accent family across the experience. Do not combine it with neon purple, electric blue, or chartreuse accents.

## Typography

- Display and interface: Geist, weights 440-650.
- Technical labels: Geist Mono, weights 500-600.
- System fallbacks: Arial for sans serif, monospace for technical labels.
- Headlines use tight tracking and compact line height. Body copy remains generous and readable.

## Layout and imagery

- Favor large whitespace, asymmetry, strong alignment, and a restrained density.
- Use soft 16 px surfaces and pill-shaped interactive controls.
- Photography should feel architectural, premium, and operationally credible.
- Branded visual accents may use mint reflections or light, but never broad neon glows.

## Voice

- Concrete before clever.
- Lead with time saved, work removed, or service improved.
- Avoid hype words and vague claims.
- Speak to owners and operators in plain business language.
