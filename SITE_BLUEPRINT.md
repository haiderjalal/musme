# Musme website blueprint

## Positioning

Musme is not presented as a list of AI tools. It is positioned as the intelligent operating layer for owner-led businesses: one partner that can remove manual work, create content, and build the digital products customers use.

Primary promise: **Less busywork. More business.**

Primary audiences:

- Healthcare operators who need to reduce admin while keeping the patient experience human.
- Restaurant owners who want joined-up bookings, menus, guest communication, content, and operations.
- Business owners who know work is being repeated but do not want to assemble five different vendors.

## Information architecture

1. **Hero**: the business outcome, one contact action, and a 3D connected-system metaphor.
2. **Operational friction**: familiar manual tasks that make the problem concrete.
3. **Capability story**: automation, content, digital products, and growth operations in a scroll-led sequence.
4. **Industry focus**: separate healthcare and restaurant propositions using one bespoke editorial visual.
5. **Engagement model**: Observe, Design, Connect, Improve.
6. **Service index**: the full breadth of what Musme can deliver without turning the page into a feature dump.
7. **Final conversion**: a single, repeated contact intent.

## Visual system

- Direction: cold luxury, architectural, minimal, precise.
- Palette: smoke black, silver grey, off-white, and Musme Mint `#6BEFBC` extracted from the approved logo.
- Typography: Geist and Geist Mono through `next/font`.
- Shape rule: soft 16px surfaces and pill-shaped controls.
- Motion: hero entrance, scroll reveals, scroll-linked depth, one restrained marquee, pointer-responsive WebGL.
- Accessibility: reduced-motion fallbacks, visible focus states, high-contrast CTAs, semantic headings, descriptive image text.

## Conversion strategy

The same phrase, **Start a project**, is used for every contact action. The production version should connect this to a calendar or a short qualification form once the preferred scheduling system and inbox are confirmed.
